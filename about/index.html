Background

I'm Rohit Kumar Sah, an electrical engineer currently based in Banepa. I graduated with a BE in Electrical Engineering from Tribhuvan University, IOE Pashchimanchal Campus, in 2025, and I now teach as an Electrical Engineering Instructor at Banepa Polytechnic Institute.

My degree work was in power system control and simulation. I was the project head of my five-member final-year team, and I did most of the simulation work and calculation tables for the project, which compared PID, ANFIS and lead-lag stabilizers in MATLAB/Simulink. Day to day, I do the calculations behind electrical designs: how much load a house will draw, which breaker and cable suit each circuit, and how big a solar and battery system needs to be.

I'm early in my career and I haven't completed paid site projects yet. What I offer is careful, well-documented calculation work, and teaching has made me good at explaining the reasoning so you can check it yourself. I work remotely and do not do site visits. Where a job needs a registered engineer's stamp or an on-site inspection, I will tell you at the start.
