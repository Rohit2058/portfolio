How I work

  1. 1. **Brief.** You send a plan, appliance list or description. I ask only what I need.

  2. 2. **Assumptions.** I write down every assumption and agree it with you before calculating.

  3. 3. **Calculation.** I prepare the sheets or drawings, with formulas you can inspect.

  4. 4. **Review.** I walk you through the results and fix anything that doesn't match your site.

  5. 5. **Handover.** You receive the files and a short summary.
